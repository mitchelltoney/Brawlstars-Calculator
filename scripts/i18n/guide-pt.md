# Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR) translation guide — brawlcalculator.com

Audience: Brazilian Brawl Stars players (Brazil is the site's #1 country). Write natural pt-BR in
the register of a good Brazilian Brawl Stars guide or creator: concise, confident, gamer vocabulary,
no stiff literal calques, no European-Portuguese forms. Use "você" if the reader is addressed.

Keep EXACTLY as written in English (never translate, never change spelling/case):
- Every name listed in the chunk's "protected" array (brawlers, gadgets, star powers, maps, modes).
- Any other capitalized in-game proper name: Super names, attack names, hypercharges, pets/summons/
  turrets (e.g. Scrappy, Bruce, Porters), and stat labels such as "CounterPower 19".
- Possessives move to Portuguese word order: "Edgar's punches" → "os socos do Edgar".
