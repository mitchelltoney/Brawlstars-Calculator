Juju Counters

Best picks against Juju in Brawl Stars ranked · updated October 2026

Best counters to Juju

  • Bolt

    Rolls through Juju's lobbed shots at full speed, and Bouncy Ball vaults her walls to slam her before Gris-Gris can intercept.

  • Brock

    Outranges Juju's short artillery reach with long-range rockets, while Rocket Fuel blasts away the walls she lobs from.

  • Shade

    Strikes Juju through the walls she hides behind, then closes in Incorporeal Form while Hardened Hoodie blunts her damage.

  • Nori

    Closes on Juju with His Mothers Son speed bursts, then roots her with Gonna Need A Bigger Net before Gris-Gris can help.

  • Trunk

    Reveals Juju's grass invisibility with Colony Scouts, then rides ant-boosted speed through her lobs to spin at close range.

  • Tick

    Outranges Juju on open ground with lingering mines, controlling lanes beyond her standard artillery reach.

  • Larry

    Outranges Juju's ground throws and adds Lawrie's pressure, forcing her to spend attacks clearing a second threat.

  • El Primo

    Out-tanks Juju's artillery and leaps over her wall cover, turning her range advantage into a close-range punch fight.

Also weak to: Wallbreaker Assassin

Juju is a strong pick against

  • Charlie

    Bombards Charlie's Spiders from behind cover and forces her yo-yo to divide attention with Gris-Gris.

  • Emz

    Threatens Emz from beyond walls as Gris-Gris enters her vulnerable angle and hinders escape.

  • Gale

    Lobs elemental shots over Gale's cover while Gris-Gris drags itself in to soak his snowball spread from another angle.

  • Meg

    Bombards Meg's slow Mecha over walls with elemental shots, while Gris-Gris splits her barrage between two targets.

  • Nita

    Lobs shots over Nita's bear to hit her directly, as Gris-Gris occupies the bear and Numbing Needles slows its chase.

  • RT

    Lobs elemental shots over RT's cover while Gris-Gris drags itself onto his stationary split legs and pins them down.
