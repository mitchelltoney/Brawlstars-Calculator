Cosmo Counters

Best picks against Cosmo in Brawl Stars ranked · updated November 2026

Best counters to Cosmo

  • Shade

    Hides inside walls during Incorporeal Form and hugs Cosmo through cover at close range, where his orbiting planets deal minimum damage.

  • Wendy

    Shields herself with every Blow Dryer blast and blunts the homing focus fire of his Super with Planet Protector's damage absorption.

  • Amber

    Out-damages his slow two-second reload with a continuous flame stream, while Fire Fluid puddles deny the spots he holds for full-range shots.

  • Nori

    Roots Cosmo with Gonna Need A Bigger Net, then dives to point-blank range where his planets deal minimum damage and reload too slowly.

  • Bibi

    Walks through his weak close-range orbit with tank health, as Home Run speed closes the gap and Batting Stance shields the approach.

  • Mortis

    Dashes through gaps between his spreading planets to reach point-blank range, punishing the slow reload before another volley is ready.

  • Gus

    Shields the teammate tagged by Gravitational Pull with his Super, absorbing homing focus fire while spirit healing outlasts slow poke.

  • Mico

    Leaps over his orbiting planets while invulnerable, then Monkey Business steals the ammo that his slow reload struggles to replace.
