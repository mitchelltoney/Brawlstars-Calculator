Best picks against Trunk in Brawl Stars ranked · updated November 2026

Best counters to Trunk

  • Amber

    Burns Trunk from beyond the short spin with continuous flames, kiting backward so the ant-boosted chase never reaches her.

  • El Primo

    Out-damages Trunk at close range, while Suplex Supplement interrupts their delayed spin before it lands.

  • RT

    Answers Trunk's ant-boosted dive with Out Of Line, bursting them with overlapping head and leg blasts at point-blank range.

  • Frank

    Outranges Trunk's short spin and out-tanks them up close, then stuns them before ant-boosted damage wins the exchange.

  • Bull

    Bursts down Trunk at point-blank range, where their circular attack deals weaker center damage.

  • Draco

    Outranges Trunk's short spin with Lance Stab's higher-damage tip, then melts them with continuous dragon flames.

Trunk is a strong pick against

  • Alli

    Exposes Alli's stealth approach with Colony Scouts, then out-tanks her dash attacks as Worker Ants turns early hits into healing.

  • Gigi

    Outlasts Gigi's spinning damage, using broader attack coverage and ant-enhanced damage to dominate their shared close range.

  • Juju

    Exposes Juju's grass invisibility with Colony Scouts, then uses ant-boosted speed to close through her lobs and spin.

  • Wendy

    Turns Wendy's chip damage into Super charge, then propels into her and spins through the teammates gathered around her generator.
