Best picks against Willow in Brawl Stars ranked · updated October 2026

Best counters to Willow

  • Shade

    Hits Willow through the walls she lobs from, while Hardened Hoodie blunts her curse damage during Incorporeal approaches.

  • Mico

    Leaps over Willow's lobbed curses and lands beside her, stealing ammo with Monkey Business before she can land her Super.

  • Larry

    Overwhelms Willow with double explosions while Lawrie forces her to divide each lobbed curse between two targets.

  • Tick

    Outranges Willow with lingering mines lobbed over walls, controlling her lanes from beyond the reach of her lantern.

  • Barley

    Out-sustains Willow's delayed poison in artillery trades because Medical Use restores health with every attack.

  • Buzz

    Bypasses Willow's wall cover with his grapple, then stuns and bursts her before she can stack delayed poison.

Also weak to: Thrower Assassin
