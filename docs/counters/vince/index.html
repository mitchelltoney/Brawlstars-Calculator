Vince Counters

Best picks against Vince in Brawl Stars ranked · updated October 2026

Best counters to Vince

  • Amber

    Matches his range with a continuous flame stream that is far easier to land than his single coffee shot, outdamaging him in long lane trades.

  • Wendy

    Shields her team from his marked follow-up shots with her shield generator, while Green Grenade cuts off his Bug Bandage healing mid-fight.

  • Bo

    Slightly outranges him with more health and wide exploding arrows, winning long-range trades before his moth collection can grow.

  • Shade

    Phases through walls in Incorporeal Form to reach him, while Hardened Hoodie blunts his single-shot damage and he has no escape tool.

  • Gus

    Outranges his coffee shots and shields the marked teammate with Spooky, absorbing the follow-up damage his Super is meant to set up.

  • Damian

    Turns his poke into Super charge with a huge health pool, then leaps in with a mosh pit that Vince has no dash or knockback to escape.

Also weak to: Assassin

Vince is a strong pick against

  • Barley

    Hits Barley behind cover with a wall-piercing Super and over-wall Hot Coffee, and outranges his slow bottles in open lanes.

  • Berry

    Outranges Berry by about two tiles, landing heavy coffee shots through his slow reload before melted ice cream can connect.

  • Nita

    Outranges Nita by over two tiles, chunking her with heavy coffee shots while denying the hits she needs to summon her bear.
