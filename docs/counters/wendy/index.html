Wendy Counters

Best picks against Wendy in Brawl Stars ranked · updated November 2026

Best counters to Wendy

  • Ash

    Out-tanks Wendy's low sustained damage while his Super's robot rats soak her non-piercing shots and Rage builds toward harder smashes.

  • Amber

    Pierces Wendy and her grouped teammates with continuous flames from slightly longer range, outpacing the small shield each Blow Dryer hit restores.

  • Shade

    Hides inside walls during Incorporeal Form where Blow Dryer cannot reach, then hugs Wendy through cover to punish her low health.

  • Trunk

    Charges their Super from the chip damage Wendy deals, then propels into her and spins through the teammates clustered in her generator.

  • Emz

    Blankets Wendy's grouped team in lingering hairspray, where Bad Karma's ramping damage wears down her shields and generator at once.

  • Rosa

    Out-tanks Wendy's modest damage behind a damage-reducing Super, then wins the close-range trade with rapid punches.

Also weak to: Tank

Wendy is a strong pick against

  • Bo

    Absorbs Bo's mine bursts with her shield generator and restores shields on every hit, outlasting the chip from his arrow volleys.

  • Cosmo

    Out-trades Cosmo by shielding herself with every Blow Dryer blast, while Planet Protector absorbs the homing fire his Super sets up.

  • Crow

    Sustains through shields rather than healing, so Crow's poison barely dents her durability while each hit restores protection against his chip.

  • Nita

    Outranges Nita's shockwaves, shields teammates against her bear, and uses Green Grenade to cut off Bear With Me healing.

  • Poco

    Cancels Poco's team healing with Green Grenade, slowing his grouped allies and stopping their recovery during a focused push.

  • Shelly

    Kites Shelly from long Blow Dryer range and soaks her point-blank shotgun burst with refreshed shields and the shield generator.

  • Vince

    Blunts Vince's marked burst with her shield generator, while Green Grenade slows him and cuts off his Bug Bandage healing mid-fight.
