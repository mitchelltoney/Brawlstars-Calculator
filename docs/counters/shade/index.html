Shade Counters

Best picks against Shade in Brawl Stars ranked · updated October 2026

Best counters to Shade

  • Doug

    Wins the close-range duel Shade must take, as Self Service heals Doug with every Snack Attack he lands.

  • Jacky

    Out-tanks Shade at point-blank range and attacks through walls, denying the cover its short range depends on.

  • RT

    Meets Shade's close dive in split form, where both halves blast it while Recording cuts the damage from its hugs.

  • Amber

    Melts Shade with a continuous flame stream as it closes, then Dancing Flames punishes the point-blank range its hugs require.

  • Frank

    Out-tanks and outranges Shade's short swings, then destroys wall cover with his Super to expose its approach.

  • Lou

    Controls Shade's short-range approach with freeze buildup, repeatedly interrupting its pressure before center hits can connect.

Also weak to: Tank

Shade is a strong pick against

  • Brock

    Slips through walls in Incorporeal Form to reach Brock, punishing him with hugs whenever Rocket Laces is down.

  • Cosmo

    Slips through walls in Incorporeal Form to reach Cosmo, then hugs him at close range where his orbiting planets deal minimum damage.

  • Juju

    Hits Juju through her protective walls, then closes in Incorporeal Form as Hardened Hoodie reduces her incoming damage.

  • Meg

    Phases past Meg's Mecha pressure through walls, punishing her fragile base form with center hugs before a remount.

  • Mina

    Bypasses Mina's projectile wall with cover-piercing attacks while Hardened Hoodie reduces her close-range combo damage.

  • Moe

    Meets Moe's Driller approach with hugs through cover, then lands Jump Scare's brief fear to deny his escape.

  • Mortis

    Endures Mortis's dash combo through Hardened Hoodie, forcing extra ammo before he can escape.

  • Vince

    Slips through walls in Incorporeal Form to catch Vince, who has no dash or knockback to stop the dive once Shade arrives.

  • Wendy

    Waits inside walls during Incorporeal Form beyond Wendy's straight shots, then hugs her through cover before shields can refresh.

  • Willow

    Strikes Willow through the walls protecting her lobs, using Hardened Hoodie to blunt curse damage while closing in Incorporeal Form.
