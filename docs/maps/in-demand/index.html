Brawl Ball

In Demand

An open Brawl Ball pitch of short scattered walls, sparse bush and fenced spawn fronts, where midfield control, shields and durable carriers win.

Decent data

S tier — best picks

  • Amber

    Long flame streams cover the open midfield and burn defenders out of the small bush tufts near goal.

  • Wendy

    Shields on carriers and teammates make open-field pushes past the short wall pieces hard to stop.

  • Ash

    Few walls to kite around, so his health and growing Rage overpower midfield fights for the ball.

  • Shade

    Wall phasing slips past the scattered wall pieces and fenced spawn fronts to flank exposed defenders.

  • Vince

    Long, fast-reloading coffee shots control the open midfield, and marked defenders fall before goal.

Best bans

  • Amber

    One of the strongest Brawl Ball picks right now, and her fire controls a midfield few teams can contest.

  • Wendy

    Shielded ball pushes are very hard to stop on a pitch with this little cover to fight from.

  • Shade

    Walls barely slow him, so defenders get flanked from any angle, and he wins Brawl Ball at a top rate.

  • Vince

    Brand-new and topping this map; his marked targets go down too fast for most defenses to hold.

    Hard to stop once enraged, and the open midfield gives kiting picks few walls to hide behind.
