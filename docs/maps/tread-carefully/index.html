Tread Carefully

A wall-free Hot Zone map where every hedge, crate and mailbox breaks, so long bush strips, sustain and fire control decide both zones.

Decent data

S tier — best picks

  • Amber

    Flames sweep the long bush strips, and with no permanent walls defenders cannot hide from her fire on the zones.

  • Wendy

    Planet Protector shields the whole team inside a zone, replacing the cover that breakable hedges cannot provide.

  • Ash

    Zone brawls build Rage fast, and his durability wins close fights once the breakable hedges are shot away.

  • Juju

    Elementalist turns the long bush strips into invisible firing spots for lobs and Gris-Gris pressure on the zone.

  • Gus

    Spirit heals and ally shields keep zone holders standing in open ground after the hedges are destroyed.

A tier — strong picks

Cosmo Shade Trunk Damian Chuck

B tier — solid picks

Vince Finx Starr Nova Draco Nori

Best bans

  • Amber

    Her fire owns both zones and the bush strips, and the map has no solid wall to fight her from.

  • Wendy

    Shielded zone holds are very hard to break without dedicated burst, and she wins this map across the board.

  • Juju

    Invisible in the long bushes and lobbing from safety, she is hard to find and harder to punish.

  • Ash

    Once enraged he bullies every close zone fight, and few picks can kite him after the hedges fall.

  • Gus

    His heals and ally shields stretch every zone fight, and he slots into almost any winning team here.
