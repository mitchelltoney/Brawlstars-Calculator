Pump It Up

A walled Brawl Ball map with long bush corridors beside each goal and wall-lined lanes, rewarding bush control, shields and close-lane brawlers.

Decent data

S tier — best picks

  • Amber

    Flames sweep the long bush corridors beside each goal, flushing out hidden defenders and carriers.

  • Shade

    Phases through the long wall lines to flank goal defenders and catch carriers hiding in the bush corridors.

  • Wendy

    Her shield generator lets the team push through the wall-lined lanes and hold narrow goal approaches.

  • Ash

    Wall-lined lanes force close fights, where his health and building Rage win the ball back.

  • Gus

    Spirit heals and ally shields keep pushers alive through the bush-lined lanes toward goal.

B tier — solid picks

Best bans

  • Amber

    Her fire owns the bush corridors and lanes, so few drafts can approach the goal safely.

  • Wendy

    Shielded pushes through the narrow walled lanes are very hard to stop without heavy burst.

  • Ash

    Once enraged he bullies every close lane fight, and few picks can kite him between these walls.

  • Shade

    Phases through the long wall lines to flank goal defenders, and he stays a top Brawl Ball pick.

  • Gus

    His spirit heals and ally shields keep pushers alive in every lane, and he fits almost any team.
