Gem Grab

Dungeon Train

An open mine inside a ring of rail track, with almost no hard cover and huge side bushes, where shields, healing and speed decide gem control.

Early estimates

S tier — best picks

  • Wendy

    Her shield generator protects the gem carrier inside the open track ring, where almost no walls exist.

  • Gus

    Spirit heals and ally shields keep the carrier alive in the wide-open mine with little cover nearby.

  • Amber

    Wide flames burn through the huge side bushes and deny the open mine to enemy collectors.

  • Bolt

    Clear rows and the open track ring let him hit top speed quickly, then crash onto exposed carriers.

  • Ash

    With few walls to kite around, his health and Rage dominate close fights over the gem mine.

A tier — strong picks

Cosmo Pam Vince Starr Nova Bo

B tier — solid picks

Damian Finx Mina Max Clancy

Best bans

  • Wendy

    Shielded carriers in the open ring are very hard to burst down before the countdown ends.

  • Bolt

    Clear lanes let him reach full speed at will and pick off carriers before support can react.

  • Amber

    Her fire clears the side bushes and holds the mine, removing most safe gem-collecting routes.

  • Gus

    His heals and shields stretch every mine fight, and he fits into almost any strong Gem Grab team.

  • Ash

    Few walls stand between him and the mine, so stopping an enraged Ash takes heavy, focused damage.
