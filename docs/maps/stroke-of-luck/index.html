Stroke Of Luck

A bushy Knockout map split by a central bush column, where most walls are breakable blocks, rewarding shields, steady poke and wall-phasing flanks.

Decent data

S tier — best picks

  • Wendy

    Shields win long no-respawn trades, and she can cross the small pools to escape or reposition.

  • Vince

    Long coffee shots win trades through the clear rows, and marked targets fall before reaching cover.

  • Shade

    Phases through the breakable block walls to reach backliners hiding behind the central bush column.

  • Pearl

    Banked heat turns slow poke wars around the breakable blocks and benches into decisive burst trades.

  • Ollie

    All Eyez On Me hypnotizes bush campers up close, and his dash Super dives defenders behind block walls.

Best bans

  • Wendy

    Her shields swing every no-respawn round, and she can slip across water when a fight turns.

  • Vince

    Topping this map early; his marked targets drop too fast to retreat behind the breakable cover.

  • Shade

    Walls cannot stop him, so backliners have no safe spot on a map built from breakable blocks.

  • Pearl

    Her banked heat bursts punish anyone peeking from cover, and rushing her through open rows is costly.

  • Brock
